Yantai museum design review

The questionnaire dataset contains ratings for 15 architecture students and 15 anonymous architectural experts. Each person has 23 rating items. The student reference condition used photographs and renderings; experts drew on prior experience of the building. All reviewers saw drawings and film of the original design, followed by VR of the adjusted design.

Anonymous participant ratings.csv records exact ratings and any interval or unresolved marks. The participant codes allow within-person comparisons. Expert and student rating matrix.csv provides the same participants in wide form. Student responses include 310 exact scores, 23 confirmed intervals and 12 unscorable marks. Table 4 gives exact-score means and denominators. Table 5 reports high ratings (4 or 5) for the student group and, for Q19, for both groups. Table 6 uses within-person comparisons for the representation items in both groups and the scheme items in students. Blank expert and pooled entries indicate items outside the reported analysis. A high Q17 score indicates greater physical discomfort. The full anonymous rating matrix is retained. Interpret individual scheme or tool items using the denominators in the manuscript.

Run paper_ratings.py to reproduce the rating matrix, Tables 4–6 and student bounds. Run "Spatial measurements.py" for Table 3, the axial correlations, exterior dimensions and sightline measures. Run "Generate analytical figures.py" with NumPy and Matplotlib for Figures 1, 4 and 7–10. The ASCII CAD drawing and original graph attributes are supplied under Technical sources and in the graph CSVs. Original and adjusted visibility maps are Map 3 and Map 1; original and adjusted axial maps are zx2 and zx1.

The Unity project and Windows build are provided under VR reproduction, together with instructions and third-party notices. Individual participant scans and photographs are excluded from the public materials.
