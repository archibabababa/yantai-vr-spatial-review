# Yantai VR spatial review

**Yantai High-Tech Zone Science Popularization Museum · July 2025**

Three linked gallery volumes were revised in VR and assessed through plan analysis and feedback from 15 architecture students and 15 architectural experts.

Accompanies *Editing linked spaces in virtual reality: A museum study of layout and representation*.

## Contributions

Fan Huang designed the study, implemented the Unity controls, conducted the spatial analysis and wrote the manuscript. Yan Zhu organised the participant study; Jia Ning Luo provided academic guidance.

## Scope

Single-site study: layout, medium and presentation order changed together. Experts had site experience; students used photographs and renderings. Results concern model geometry and participant assessments, not measured visitor behaviour.
